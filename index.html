Dr. Sanchi Agrawal is a distinguished dentist and endodontist specializing in conservative dentistry and microscopic endodontics. She completed her BDS at CSMSS Dental College, Aurangabad, followed by an internship at the prestigious Government Dental College, Mumbai.

She pursued her MDS at ITS Ghaziabad, graduating as the batch topper and 4th rank university-wide. Her extensive clinical experience includes a Senior Residency at Safdarjung Hospital, Delhi, alongside hands-on clinical mastery at Fortis Healthcare.

Recognized with the Young Achievers Award from IACDE and listed among the Top 10 Endodontists in India, she advanced her expertise in Microscopic Endodontics, Smile Designing, and Implantology. At The Smile Edit, she treats every patient's smile as a unique work of art.
